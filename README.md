# haven-project
Bouncy Battlefield
Game for Haven Game Jam

A strategic simulator game where you constantly build your animal army and survive the raids by other animals. Your animal army will go on a chaotic battle every raids

This is the game where I made as the product for the game jam for Hack club Haven Singapore. Builted by NNKD - Kevin and Bui Ba Thang. In this game, you will choose an animal and start growing your army. Then raids will come when more hostile animal finds your fence and attack you. You army will fight until no more animal last. If you survive, you will grow your army and make it another day. Any dead animal will remain dead and never return afterwards. This gives you opportunity to bounce back and grow stronger

Buttons:
Space: Start the game
Q or Shift: 3x speed


Builted with Godot 4.7
